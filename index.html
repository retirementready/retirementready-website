Retirement is more than a single number.

A strong retirement plan connects the decisions that matter instead of treating them in isolation. RetirementReady is designed to help you explore the tradeoffs, compare alternatives, and understand what changes when one assumption changes another.

When can I retire?

Explore how retirement timing affects income, spending, portfolio longevity, taxes, and healthcare.

When should I claim Social Security?

Compare claiming ages and understand how one spouse's decision can affect the household and survivor.

Should I convert to Roth?

Evaluate Roth conversions in the context of taxes, RMDs, Medicare premiums, healthcare subsidies, and long-term outcomes.

How much can I spend?

Connect spending choices to sustainable withdrawals and the lifestyle you want retirement to support.

What could healthcare cost?

Account for the transition into retirement, pre-Medicare coverage, Medicare, and income-related premium effects.

What happens after one spouse dies?

Understand survivor income, taxes, spending needs, and how today's decisions may affect the spouse who remains.
