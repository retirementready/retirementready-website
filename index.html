Retirement is more than a single number.

A strong retirement plan connects the decisions that matter. RetirementReady is designed to help you compare alternatives and see how changing one assumption can affect the rest of the plan.

When can I retire?

See how retirement timing affects income, spending, taxes, healthcare, and portfolio longevity.

When should I claim Social Security?

Compare claiming ages and see how one spouse's decision can affect the household and survivor.

Should I convert to Roth?

Evaluate Roth conversions alongside taxes, RMDs, Medicare premiums, and healthcare subsidies.

How much can I spend?

Connect spending choices to sustainable withdrawals and the lifestyle you want to support.

What could healthcare cost?

Plan for pre-Medicare coverage, Medicare, and income-related premium effects.

What happens after one spouse dies?

Understand survivor income, taxes, spending needs, and the impact of today's decisions.
